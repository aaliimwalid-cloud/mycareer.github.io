Multi-channel sales pipeline & dashboard

Problem

Sales came from a delivery app, self-order kiosks and two catering platforms. Most had no API, so data arrived as CSV attachments or plain email bodies, each with its own format and its own names for the same outlet.

Built

Scheduled n8n workflows that read each report, parse the HTML or CSV, keep only confirmed orders, map every outlet to one code, and load clean rows into PostgreSQL. Product reports keep the item-and-modifier hierarchy so add-ons stay linked to their item. A custom dashboard sits on top, with company Google sign-in and row-level security: owners see every outlet, managers see their own.

Result

One daily view of retail and catering sales across every channel, replacing manual copying from inboxes.

Email & CSV reports→n8n parse & filter→PostgreSQL→Dashboard

Central Kitchen Ordering System

Problem

Outlets ordered stock from the central kitchen through chat messages. There was no reliable record of what was ordered, changed or delivered, and invoices were put together by hand.

Built

A web app used daily by each outlet and the kitchen: store-locked logins, an order lifecycle (confirm, amend, void), sequential invoice numbers and printing, stock counts, store-to-store transfers, reports, real-time sync across devices and an accounting-ready CSV export. I later rebuilt its security, replacing client-side PINs with one auth account per store, custom token claims and row-level security, so each outlet sees only its own orders while the kitchen sees all.

Result

Ordering, invoicing and stock counts in one system with a full audit trail.

Outlet order→Kitchen confirm / amend→PostgreSQL→Invoice & accounts export

Ingredient costing & invoice automation

Problem

Supplier invoices arrived as scanned documents across several outlets, so ingredient prices and spend were hard to track and recipes could not be costed with confidence.

Built

OCR extraction of supplier invoices into structured data, then Python scripts to deduplicate ingredients across outlets and maintain an invoice log and a single ingredient master. I extended it into a procurement prototype with supplier ordering, three-way matching of purchase order, invoice and supplier statement, and a recipe costing builder.

Result

One price list per ingredient, used for recipe costing and stock planning for new menu items.

Scanned invoices→OCR→Python clean & dedupe→Ingredient master→Recipe costs

The forecasts feed each outlet's monthly sales target.

Feasibility study · Mar 2026

New outlet financial feasibility

Should the group open its fifth outlet in north-east Singapore? I sized the market bottom-up (TAM, SAM, SOM) from government population and planning data, scanned local competitors, and built the revenue model from three months of the network's own transaction data rather than industry benchmarks. Then I ran the store through unit economics, CAPEX payback, cash survival and free-cash-flow conversion tests across bear, base and bull cases, flagging every assumption still to be replaced with actuals.

Outcome: recommended as feasible. The outlet opened in April 2026.
